Model Card: Connect Buddy

Purpose
Match high school students with eligible opportunities based on their interests.
Data used
Only the interests you type and the public listing database. No grades, ID or location.
Verification
Programs are checked for legitimacy, high school eligibility and no student-paid fees.
Limitations
AI can be wrong. Confirm details with the organization and a trusted adult. Deadlines change yearly.
Fairness
We favor free or paid programs so money is never a barrier.
Prototype note
This demo uses simple keyword matching, not a live model.
